Structural decipherment of the Liber Loagaeth

What it is, how it was found, and how anyone can reproduce it. · Base transcription: Sloane MS 3189 · Analysis: loagaeth-analysis · seed 1583 · full text in docs/PESQUISA_LOAGAETH.md

Thesis in one sentence. The Loagaeth is not a text with meaning: it is a field of names generated by a small combinatorial automaton (prefix·root·suffix), read in rows, with an architecture (name density peaks mid-book and fades at the end) and a genealogy (the names form families that marry). It is self-contained (it does not bridge into the Watchtowers/Heptarchy/Governors) and carries only a thin layer of real language (~24 words). What does not exist is a word-for-word translation of the names.

Method — the discipline of the null

Each finding follows the template Question · Method · Null · Result · Reading · Reproduce. z = standard deviations above chance (z>3 strong; z>6 decisive). Project rule: no pattern counts until it beats a null; pareidolia is the enemy. Refuted findings stay on record (docs/RESEARCH_LOG.md, #NNN).

Part I — The nature of the book

A field of names, not prose #332

Question
does folio 1 chain words into sentences?
Method
conditional entropy H(next|current) of the 1a+1b corpus.
Null
shuffle the word order.
Result
H real 2.84 vs null 2.86, z=−1.3. Recurring bigrams 12 vs 2 (z=+6.7), but word order carries no information.
Reading
there is a lexicon, but no sentence syntax. The book lists; it does not sentence.

The "particles" are generator pieces, not grammar #334

Question
are the ~162 short tokens a function-word class or name pieces?
Method
positional bias, phonotactics (B vs glossed A vs names C), and tiling the 90 names with the piece inventory.
Result
zero positional bias (like the names); B groups with names (cos 0.806 vs 0.700); the 90 names tile 85% of letters, 39/90 fully, vs null 36%/0.4 (z=+5.5).
Reading
particles = onomastic morphology (affixes/fragments). Folio 1 is the workshop page.
Reproduce
a169_particle_function.py, a170_name_tiling.py

Not a natural language (by linguistic laws) #351

Method
Zipf, Heaps, entropy, NCD vs Latin/Hebrew/Greek/English/random.
Result
Zipf slope −0.36 (language ≈ −1); Heaps β≈0.97 (vocabulary never saturates = name list); entropy higher than any real language; by compression (NCD) it does not cluster with languages — it sits near random.
Reading
a high-entropy name field, not an idiom.
Reproduce
a187_language_battery.py

What we did NOT find (limits)

• The word-gloss of each name ("NOCHA = X"): there is no Rosetta Stone; Dee never translated the tables.
• A special Semitic grammar / the Forge's predictive power: REFUTED by strong controls (it is order-2-3 phonotactics; a Markov-3 reproduces it). Decomposition is description; generation is faithful construction, not a deciphered tongue.
• Residual circularity: density and row-reading inherit that names were derived by row. The most solid results are those independent of this: the gradient, the dead column, the border, the 26-28 seam, the genealogy, the bridges, astrology.
