# Verified patterns (72 hypotheses)

72 hypotheses about the Watchtowers, the Calls, the governors, the Heptarchia and the
gematria were tested by script, each against chance and against the way the tables are
built. **Confirmed: 31** - but the large majority is real structure that the authors
(Dee, the Golden Dawn, Crowley) deliberately designed, not new hidden code.

The most solid single result: **ZAX without the L spells the Table of Union letter by
letter** (20/20, p ~ 7.5e-18) - but the *True & Faithful Relation* p.179 already says the
cross was written from those names, so it is documented, not a discovery.

In no case did a hidden code appear that the naked eye could not already see. What the
scripts did was separate structural pattern from pareidolia.

## Counts

| status | count | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | 31 | holds in the data (often by the author's own construction) |
| Trivial | 35 | true, but follows from grid geometry or a declared rule |
| Refuted | 4 | fails its null |
| Inconclusive | 2 | not decidable from available data |

Convention: "confirmed" means the statement stands in the data, **not** that it is new.
"chance" = the baseline comparison. All scripts lived in the original `lab/verif/`.

## Confirmed (notable)

- **H01 ZAX without L = Union.** Spells EXARP HCOMA NANTA BITOM (20/20, p=7.5e-18).
  Circular: documented in the TFR. Real, not new.
- **H02 Union 4x5 and prefixes.** Archangel = Tower letter + kerub (16/16; the sub-element
  rule gives 4/16). Fixed a bug: the archangel prefix comes from the Tower.
- **H07 King in an 8-house ring.** The given order reads the 4 kings; only 1 of 40,320
  permutations does (p=2.4e-13). Robust; the king is the strong part.
- **H12 Egyptian gods = a function of the elements.** 256 squares, 0 conflicts - an
  explicit GD design rule (Mathers), not Dee's code.
- **H25 Digits 2, 3, 6 in the numbers.** Servants of the 91 governors: 2/3/6 make 59% of
  digits vs 30% expected (p < 3e-4); 62/91 numbers contain a 6. **The strongest and least
  explained pattern in the whole set.**
- **H34 Carmara/Baligon/Blumaza/Hagonel/Bagenol identities.** Three incompatible
  identities for Carmara confirmed in the text (p~0.0013).
- **H53 Gematria meta-test.** Real Enochian names do NOT beat shuffled names in equal-value
  hits (10 vs 11.2 expected; p=0.67-0.93). This is why most gematria falls into "trivial."
- (Also confirmed, mostly by construction: H03, H08, H14, H16, H17, H18, H21-H24, H27,
  H37, H38, H48, H54-H56, H58, H59, H66, H68, H70-H72.)

## Trivial (35)

True but forced by the grid or a declared rule: H04, H05, H06, H09, H10, H11, H15, H19,
H20, H26, H28-H33, H35, H39-H47, H49, H50, H52, H60, H63-H65, H67, H69. Most gematria
"identities" live here - once you pick the targets afterward, the coincidences are
expected by chance.

## Refuted (4)

- **H13 YHVH order + qualifier.** The four orders hold only for Air and Water (a Latin
  square); Earth and Fire use different orders. The element-count qualifier errs 49.7%.
- **H36 "only Mercury shifted" in the planetary orders.** Holds only for kings/insignia,
  which are not independent. The Sons-of-Light metals do not follow it.
- **H51 saturation of 7 and 13.** Servants: 13 multiples of 7 (expected 13.0; p=0.55).
  The only excess is structural (7 letters by construction). The 2/3/6 digit pattern (H25)
  is far stronger.
- **H62 reversal as a formal device.** Corpus: 2.13% of 4-7 letter words have a valid
  reverse vs 1.54% null (p=0.167). Enochian: 0 reverse pairs among 686 forms. The only
  documented reversal is Dee's back-to-front dictation of Calls 1-4.

## Inconclusive (2)

- **H57 divergent attributions between sources.** Only East is common across arrangements.
- **H61 patterns on the Tree of Life.** The exact figures the claims need are not in the
  text (numbers appear only in the diagrams).
