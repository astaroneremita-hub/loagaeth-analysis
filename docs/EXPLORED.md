# What we explored: everything we tried

This is the honest ledger. It lists what held up against a strong null model, what we
had to throw out, and the broad engine sweep across the whole Enochian system. The
headline, stated up front so nothing here oversells: **no hidden code appeared that the
naked eye could not already see.** What the tools do well is separate real structure
from coincidence - including our own favorite ideas, several of which died.

## Part 1 - The Loagaeth (layer A, `src/`)

### Held up (beat the strong null or are true by inspection)
- **Catalog of names, not prose.** Type/token ratio ~0.81 on the word-spaced leaves;
  repetition structure with no prose-like long-range grammar.
- **Scale-free name-network.** ~4,300 recurring-6-gram nodes; heavy-tailed degree
  distribution, a few dominant hubs (the "hive").
- **CHASTOR, the master stem.** 184 occurrences across 35 of 48 tables (72.9%);
  NOCHASTOR adds 61 across 27. **CHUSTOR** is its A/U vowel twin, appearing in 4 of the
  13 tables that lack CHASTOR.
- **Productive morphology.** The stem takes a closed prefix inventory
  (NO- 77, NE- 24, ZI- 18, NA- 12, OL- 11) producing a clear paradigm (NOCHASTOR,
  NECHASTOR, ZICHASTOR, NOCHASTORAD, ...). The same affixes recur on other stems
  (-GETH, -DAX families). This is the most language-like behavior in the manuscript.
- **Per-table signature names.** Each of the 48 tables has its own distinctive name
  (DOMNAG, ...; HZEMBL and DAHOXI, first listed here, cross word boundaries and are withdrawn - a51); none belong to the CHASTOR family.
- **A small real bridge to the Calls.** 23 of 2,619 word-spaced tokens exactly match
  attested Angelical forms (~10x chance); four carry known meanings (IAD god, GAH
  spirits, ORS darkness, URAN elders).

### Thrown out (tested, failed its strong null)
- **Mirror / reversal.** 102 reverse-pairs looked huge against a letter-shuffle (3.3)
  but died against the Markov-3 surrogate (92.8, z~0.95). A syllable-pool artifact. An
  independent corpus check agrees (0 reverse pairs among ~686 attested forms).
- **Semitic triliteral roots.** Words-per-consonant-skeleton ratio ~1.3-1.4, English-like,
  nowhere near the >2 of root-and-pattern languages. The morphology is concatenative.
- **Call vocabulary as a decode key.** ~3.4% substring overlap - chance.
- **Gebofal weekly reordering** as a reading order. No signal.
- **Four geometric models** - 3D pentagonal solid, honeycomb, tesseract, a "CHASTOR
  cube" generating names like a planetary square. All pareidolia; none beat null.
- **Divine-name ciphers** (YHVH / MAD / Tetragrammaton as a grid key). Nothing.

## Part 2 - The whole Enochian system (layer B, the engine)

The recursive engine ran 72 hypotheses across the Great Table, the Calls, the 91
governors, the Heptarchia and the gematria systems, each against its own null. Counts:

| status | count | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| confirmed | 31 | the statement holds in the data... |
| trivial | 35 | ...but true by grid geometry or a declared rule |
| refuted | 4 | fails its null |
| inconclusive | 2 | not decidable from the data we have |

The crucial honesty: of the 31 "confirmed," almost all are **by construction** - things
Dee or the Golden Dawn openly built, or forced by the grid. The clearest example,
**ZAX-minus-the-L spelling the Table of Union** (20/20, astronomically significant), is
real but documented in Dee's *True & Faithful Relation* (he built the cross from those
names). The Egyptian-god square, the king-ring order, the senior orderings: all gorgeous,
all documented construction, not hidden code. The meta-test on gematria (H53) shows real
names do **not** beat shuffled names in collision counts - which is why most gematria
"hits" fall into "trivial."

### The one unexplained thread
The single strong pattern with **no construction explanation**: the digits **2, 3 and 6
dominate the numbers Dee attached to the 91 governors** - 59% of all digits vs 30%
expected (p < 3e-4), and 62 of 91 numbers contain a 6. We do not know why. If the Loagaeth
(or the wider system) has a real key, our bet is it is partly **numeric**, and this is the
loose thread worth pulling.

### Engine probes that got downgraded by their own hardened null
- **3-6-9 digital roots** in name values (S7): concentrated, but the hardened null
  (shuffle letter->value) shows it belongs to the Golden Dawn value system, not the names.
- **Per-Tower sefirotic signature** (S8): the separation comes from the four Tables having
  different letter compositions (a known fact); the modal roots do not match the elements'
  traditional numbers. Not a sefirotic code.
- **Loagaeth k-grams shared with the Towers** (S10): falls against a Markov pseudo-Loagaeth
  - it is just the common Enochian alphabet.

## Part 3 - Where this leaves us

The Loagaeth's skeleton is clear: a catalog of names, a scale-free network, one master
stem with real word-formation, one proper name per table. Structure is not meaning. Every
internal key we tried to turn structure into meaning failed its null. The only path left
is an **external anchor**: the attested sense of a single Loagaeth name (CHASTOR is the
highest-value target, since it governs most of the book). One real meaning would let us
test it as a key across the 35 tables - a real meaning leaves a trace in the neighboring
words; an imagined one does not.
